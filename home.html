About Us

Welcome to THEOS IMPEX

A trusted name in international trade, based in the coastal city of Kanyakumari, India — connecting the best of Indian products with the world.

THEOS IMPEX is a leading import-export company headquartered in Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu, India. We specialize in sourcing and supplying a wide range of high-quality products including fresh agricultural produce, spices, seafood, metal scraps, and recyclable materials to markets across the globe.

Our commitment to quality, transparent dealings, and reliable logistics has earned us the trust of partners worldwide. We work closely with local farmers, manufacturers, and suppliers to ensure every product meets international standards.

At THEOS IMPEX, we don't just trade products — we build lasting relationships that bridge markets and cultures across continents.
